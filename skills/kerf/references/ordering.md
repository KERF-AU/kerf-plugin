# Ordering, prices and policies

Source: kerf.au/guides/ordering and kerf.au/terms, September 2026.

## Instant pricing

- Upload at https://kerf.au/quote; the price appears in seconds, calculated from the actual geometry of the part. Changing material, thickness, finish or quantity reprices live. No account is needed to price or order.
- Every quote gets a number (format KF-2609-7K3Q). Prices are in Australian dollars and include GST. Delivery is separate, added at checkout. An ABN tax invoice comes with every order.
- Prices are held for 14 days from the date on the quote. A checkout that is started but not paid is kept for 7 days and then closed; the quote stays open for the full 14 days, so Checkout can simply be clicked again.
- Never state a price yourself. The live quote is the only price.

## What moves the price

1. Material and thickness: thicker cuts slower and costs more.
2. Cut length: the jet is priced by the distance it travels; every hole and slot adds to it. A detailed, lacy part costs more than a plain one of the same size.
3. Quantity: parts on the same order share the setup, so the unit price falls as quantity rises. Worth trying a few numbers.
4. Finish: as cut, or deburring on parts up to 200 mm.

## Review before cutting

A person reviews every file before it is cut. If a problem the automatic check missed is found, KERF contacts the customer before cutting. If an instant price turns out to be wrong (a mis-measured file, a mis-priced material or thickness), KERF tells the customer before cutting and gives the corrected price; the customer accepts it or cancels that part for a full refund. KERF never cuts and charges more without asking first.

## Checkout and payment

Checkout opens KERF's online store (shop.kerf.au) with the quote's lines. Delivery is chosen there and paid with the parts. The agent never handles payment details; the person pays in the browser.

## Lead time and shipping

See services.md: cut within 9 business days of payment, delivery on top; Australia Post rates by weight; oversize orders by a separate freight quote.

## Returns and problems

Custom-cut parts cannot be returned or exchanged. Parts that arrive defective, damaged, or not matching the file and specification ordered: email hello@kerf.au within 14 days of receiving them, with photos and the order number; KERF investigates and recuts and resupplies parts that do not match the order. A part cut accurately to the file is not a defect if the design itself was wrong. Nothing in the policy limits Australian Consumer Law rights.

## Reorders and accounts

Every ordered part is saved to the customer's parts library. Sign-in is by an emailed link, no password. Reorders are a couple of clicks, priced at current rates. Uploading a file that was uploaded before reuses the existing part instead of making a copy. Quotes can be shared by link.

## Tax invoices

Every paid order has a tax invoice PDF in Order history (Orders in the quote workspace, then Tax invoice). It shows KERF CUTTING's ABN, the GST included and everything paid, delivery included. An order confirmation email arrives on payment and a second email when parts ship.

## Files and intellectual property

Design files stay the customer's. KERF claims no ownership or licence beyond what is needed to cut and deliver the parts and keep a record of the order. Files are stored securely, used only to cut the parts, never shared. Deletion of files, account or data: email hello@kerf.au.
